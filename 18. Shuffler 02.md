
Go to <a href="https://www.virustotal.com">Virustotal.com</a> and click on "Sign up" in the upper right-hand corner to create account.

<a href="https://www.virustotal.com"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-VirusTotal-007ACC?&style=for-the-badge&logo=virustotal&logoColor=white" /></a>

<br>

<img width="2880" height="1612" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2c1c6e3e-36fb-4556-9975-4fbf43e137df" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

You will receive an email to activate your account. 

Once you have signed in, click on your name in the upper right-hand corner, and then click "API Key". Click the copy icon beside your API key to copy it. It's the blurry code block.

<br>

<img width="2880" height="670" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5b3f7246-5d96-46c7-a01c-677f51c7ece0" />

<br><br>

<img width="2880" height="1620" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ca75f51c-01c9-4768-b744-96cf275a9dc3" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Return to Shuffler and click on the VirusTotal icon. Under the "Set up" tab click "Add Authentication", then paste in you API key.

Change "Auth for Virustotal_v3" to "VT-Auth" for short. Then click submit. 

<br>

<img width="2880" height="1626" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/15e3901f-d2b2-44d1-afc3-9c59f846da3f" />

<br><br>

<img width="2192" height="1404" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bbbcbd11-17e4-4a42-a536-f0064b071719" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Click on the "SHA256-Hash" icon, click the blue dot to the right of it and drag it to the "VirusTotal" icon. Then click on the plus sign in the text box under "Id" and choose "SHA256-Hash".

<br>

<img width="2850" height="1602" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b33fd453-f963-45e7-9529-d4a41cc7ea5b" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Now click "Save" on the bottom tool bar to save our workflow. Then refresh the web browser page.

<br>

<img width="1350" height="210" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1258b0f6-a1bb-4e4d-850d-e0f7c6ad1079" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

If you're web hook is no longer listening,  click it and hit "Start" under "Webhook URI".

<br>

<img width="2840" height="1572" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/30c7905d-c5b7-4b5f-b490-5666275dda34" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Return to you mimikatz PowerShell window and run it again.

<br>

<img width="2306" height="1140" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7cfaeb07-782b-45de-b7b6-6b7d9d4518f2" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Now return to Shuffler and wait a little while. A good while. You should notice the "SHA256-Hash" icon has turned green and so as the line connecting it to VirusTotal. 

<br>

<img width="2874" height="1616" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cab8b065-e9a6-4578-95ba-c58f8e77cf64" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Select the running man on the bottom toolbar.

<br>

<img width="96" height="82" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e3dd97bf-f6e9-4e27-ab63-5f55b25b67a2" />

<br><br>

<img width="1264" height="126" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c92b5ada-b61d-4198-b8ea-9dfba8f52c06" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

You should see two workflow runs. One from when we first ran mimikats during set up and another from when we just ran it after saving our workflow. I have three in my screen shot because I ran it twice after saving.

<br>

<img width="2880" height="1622" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/00f95aa7-be83-4452-90a6-b51d8a53bace" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Click on the second one. in my case it's the one in the middle with the exclamation point beside it.

Scroll down until you see "SHA256-Hash" then click on the plus sign to the left of `group_0`. You should see the hash value.

<br>

<img width="908" height="688" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd5eb73c-3c36-4982-9da3-f00f54b0f1eb" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

However, if we scroll down a little further to VirusTotal, we see a 404 status. That means our enrichment didn't work.

<br>

<img width="880" height="282" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0ac2ce55-c2ff-4979-9646-297e89058caf" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Go back to the canvas and click on VirusTotal. Delete the text in the text box under "Id" then click on the plus sign to the right. This time instead of just selecting "SHA256-Hash", hover over it and click "list". This essentially filters out everything but the sha256 hash value itself. 

<br>

<img width="2866" height="1602" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e51c9ae0-f509-47a5-af6b-431556b707ee" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Save the workflow again by clicking on save at the bottom.

<br>

<img width="1266" height="128" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/702c7d29-b958-4fff-87a2-3cc860e0b79a" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Click the running man on the bottom toolbar again, Then click on the two arrows in a circle beside "Details" to rerun the workflow.

<br>

<img width="2876" height="1616" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-11 184449" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2e8a1d44-9022-4cc2-a34a-6666d2d55cf7" />

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

Scroll down again and you will see that VirusTotal now has a status of 200.
