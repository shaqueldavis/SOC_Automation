# Un-Sync VM

### Disconnect Internet 

In order to prevent Microsoft from automatically syncing your information once you open up Microsoft Edge web browser or  OneDrive we will immediately disconnect the VM from the internet.

First turn off the Wi-Fi from your host computer, because it is the fastest and most definite method.

### Take Snapshot

I would also recommend taking a snapshot at this point. Microsoft is pretty aggressive about syncing your information. In the case that you accidentally open a Microsoft Edge web browser or OneDrive while the VM is connected, this will pretty much happen instantly. If it does, the only sure way to remove your personal info from the VM is to delete it and reinstall it from scratch. <br>
However, if we have a snap shot of the un-synced state we can always revert back.

Take a snapshot by going to: VM > Snapshot > Take Snapshot… <br>
Name your snapshot and hit `Take Snapshot`.

### Set Up Local Account

To completely disassociate your Windows VM with your Microsoft account, you can use a local user account instead.

This step will require you to turn back on your Wi-Fi and network adapter. First make sure that all web browsers are closed out in the VM just in case.
- 1. Turn on the wifi from you host computer
- 2. Reverse the steps from earlier in this section to turn you VM’s network adapter back on: VM > Settings > Network adapter

Go to: Windows start menu > Settings > Accounts > your info.

Scroll down and you will see the option to “Sign in with a local account instead” under “Account settings”. <br>
Choose that option.

<br>

# Delete synced passwords

Some of your passwords would have more than likely synced when you Microsoft account was signed in. Lets Delete them. <br>
Open Microsoft Edge. go to settings > passwords & autofill > Microsoft password manager. click on one of your passwords and enter your VM's pin to access it. Then click delete beside it.

Do this for all saved passwords.
