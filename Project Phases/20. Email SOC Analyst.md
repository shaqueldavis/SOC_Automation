# Email SOC Analyst

Now we will send an email over to the SOC analyst

Return to Shuffler and search for "Email" in the left hand menu, the same way we did VirusTotal and TheHive.

Select "Email". Then once it appears with your apps drag and drop it to your canvas, and click on it to reveal the configuration options on the right. 

The configuration should be currently set to "Send email shuffle".

Enter a valid email that you have access to under "Recipients". Enter "Mimikatz Detected" under "Subject", and put whatever you want under "Body".

Now hover over VirusTotal and drag a connection to Email.
<br>

If you go down to the tool bar at the bottom you may see that the text box to the left of save is populated. 

If so, when you hit the play button you will not receive a prompt asking you to select an argument. If not, select the most recent argument when prompted after hitting the play button.

Save your workflow and click the play button.
